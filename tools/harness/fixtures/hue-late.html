Download your data

Last updated: 1 month ago
The easiest and fastest way is your Settings & Privacy page:
Click the Me icon at the top of your homepage.
Select Settings & Privacy from the dropdown.

Under How LinkedIn uses your data, click Download.

You should only download your data from a personal computer.
This feature is currently not available on mobile.
You'll only receive the categories of data that apply to you.
LinkedIn will only provide your own personal data.
If you close your account, you'll lose access.
Some connections' email addresses may be missing.
